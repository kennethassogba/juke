# JUKE

One paddle, three opponents and a shared puck. Face three OpenAI models making decisions from the arena, or switch to classic Arcade and choose your difficulty.

Play: https://play.keff.uk/juke/
Install: https://play.keff.uk/juke/?install=1
Manifest: https://play.keff.uk/juke/manifest.webmanifest

## Controls

Drag your paddle, touch the arena or use the arrow keys. Defend the bottom goal.

## Modes and rules

- GPT-6 Luna: Decisions API, choice question. GPT-5.6 Luna: Responses, strict JSON, reasoning none. GPT-4.1 nano: Responses, strict JSON.
- See each model’s measured response time and usage cost. OpenAI play includes $0.50 per browser per day.
- Arcade has adjustable difficulty, obstacles and shareable replays.
