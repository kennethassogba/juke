# Token Factory

Build a token factory against a local computer opponent. Tap to earn credits, hire models and add machines before your rival gets there.

Play: https://play.keff.uk/token-factory/
Install: https://play.keff.uk/token-factory/?install=1
Manifest: https://play.keff.uk/token-factory/manifest.webmanifest

## Controls

Tap or hold Make tokens. Spend credits on models, harnesses and machines. No API calls or real charges.

## Modes and rules

- Reach 10,000 tokens first, or lead when the two minutes are up.
- Choose models using their dated Intelligence Index and API price references. Production and credits are game values.
- Connect a harness, upgrade your machines and share a challenge.
